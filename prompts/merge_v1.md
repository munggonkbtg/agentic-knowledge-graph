Answer one of:
- same: both names refer to the same right, benefit, defined term, procedure, role, form, system, organisation or place. A Thai name and its English translation can be the same. A shorter name and a longer name for the same thing are the same ("Short Term Disability" and "short-term disability claim", "ลาเพื่อกิจธุระอันจำเป็น" and "สิทธิลาเพื่อกิจธุระอันจำเป็น").
- different: they refer to different things, even if the words overlap. A part is not the whole ("Medical Insurance" is not "Health Insurance"); a general term is not a specific one ("leave" is not "sick leave"); two different payments are different ("ค่าล่วงเวลา" is not "ค่าล่วงเวลาในวันหยุด").
- unsure: you cannot tell from the names and passages.

Rules:
1. Judge what the names refer to, not how similar the words look.
